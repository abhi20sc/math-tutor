## 1. What this is

Astro Math Assist gives Ontario high-school students multiple-choice practice
across Grades 9 to 12, along with lessons, practice tests, and a report they
can share with a parent or tutor.

It is a **study aid**. It is not a course, it is not accredited, it does not
award credits, and using it is not a substitute for attending school or doing
the work your teacher sets. Nothing in it is a prediction of what you will
score on a real test.

## 2. Who may use it

- You need an account, and you need to give a real email address.
- **If you are under 13**, a parent or guardian has to set the account up and
  consent to it.
- Give us accurate information. Telling us the wrong grade means you get the
  wrong questions, which only wastes your own time.
- **Your account is yours alone.** Do not share your password or let someone
  else practise under your name — your report is meant to describe you, and it
  stops meaning anything if it describes two people.

If you fill in the Astro+ form, we email your parent or guardian a payment
link. By submitting that form you are confirming that **the person you have
named has agreed to be contacted about this**. Please do not enter someone's
details without asking them first.

The link expires after 30 days, and you can cancel the request from the app
before it is used.

## 5. What you may not do

- Don't try to extract, scrape, copy or republish the question bank. It took a
  long time to write and it is the whole product.
- Don't try to get at answers other than by answering questions — no probing
  the API, no automated requests, no attempts to bypass the paywall.
- Don't share your account, resell access, or use one subscription for several
  students.
- Don't upload a profile photo that is obscene, that impersonates someone, or
  that is not yours to upload.
- Don't attack the service, and don't attempt to reach anyone else's data.

If you find a security hole, please email us instead of using it. We will
thank you properly, and we will not come after you for looking.
