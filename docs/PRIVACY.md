# Privacy Policy

**Astro Math Assist**

*Last updated: 23 August 2026*

---

## In short

Astro Math Assist is a maths practice app for Ontario high-school students. To
work, it has to remember who you are and which questions you have answered.
That is essentially all it keeps.

- **Your data stays in Canada.** Our database runs in Amazon's Canadian region
  (`ca-central-1`), not in the United States.
- **We do not sell or share your information with anyone**, and there is no
  advertising in this app.
- **There are no analytics or tracking cookies.** No Google Analytics, no
  advertising pixels, no third-party scripts watching what you do.
- **We never see your card number.** Payments go through Stripe.
- **You can ask us to delete everything**, and we will.

The rest of this page is the detail behind those five sentences.

---

## 1. Who we are

Astro Math Assist is operated by STEM Labs, a private mathematics tutoring
practice based in Ontario, Canada.

For any question about your information, or to ask for a copy or a deletion,
write to **stemlabs.ca@gmail.com**. A person reads that inbox, and we aim to
answer within seven days.

## 2. What we collect, and why

We collect only what the app needs to do its job. Every item below exists
because a feature would not work without it.

### When you create an account

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Email address | To sign you in, and to send a password reset if you ask for one |
| Password | Stored only as a cryptographic hash. Nobody at STEM Labs can read it |
| Your name | So your tutor sees a name rather than an email address |
| Course and grade | To decide which question bank you see |

### While you use the app

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Every question you answer, which option you chose, whether it was right, and when | This is the product. Without it there is no progress, no medals and no report |
| Which mistake a wrong answer matched | So the app can tell you *why* an answer was wrong, and show your tutor what to go over |
| Which lessons you have opened, and roughly how long for | So the app can tick off what you have read |
| Practice test scores | To work out the percentage shown on your topic map |
| A profile photo, if you upload one | So your tutor's class list has faces on it. Entirely optional |

### If you join a class

Your tutor can see your name, your progress, your practice-test scores, and
which topics you find difficult. They can also write notes about your work,
which you can read. Your tutor cannot see your password or your payment
details.

### If you ask a parent to pay for Astro+

The Astro+ form asks for your name, grade, school board, school (optional),
and **your parent or guardian's name, email address and phone number**. We use
these only to send that one person a payment link and, if the payment does not
go through, to follow up about it. We do not add them to any mailing list.

### Payments

Stripe handles all card payments. **Card numbers never reach our servers and
we never see them.** We store only Stripe's customer reference, whether your
subscription is active, and the date it runs to. Stripe has its own privacy
policy at stripe.com/privacy.

If you pay by Interac e-Transfer, the transfer happens entirely through your
own bank. We see the sender's name and the message you attach, the same as any
other e-transfer recipient.

### Automatically

Nothing beyond what is listed above. There is no analytics, no tracking
pixel, no advertising identifier and no third-party script of any kind on
any page of this app.

Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, keeps its own short-lived
service logs of requests made to it, in the same Canadian region as the
data. We do not add to them and we do not use them for anything but
diagnosing a fault.

## 3. What we do NOT collect

We think the absences matter as much as the list above:

- **No analytics or tracking of any kind.** No Google Analytics, no Meta
  pixel, no session recording, no heatmaps, no third-party scripts.
- **No advertising**, and no advertiser has access to anything here.
- **No location data.** We do not ask for it and we do not infer it.
- **No contact list, camera roll, or microphone.** The only file the app ever
  asks for is a profile photo you choose yourself.
- **No cookies except the one that keeps you signed in.** There is no consent
  banner on this site because there is nothing to consent to.
- **No selling, renting, or sharing of your information with anyone**, for any
  purpose, ever.

## 4. Where your information lives

| Where | What is there | Country |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase (Amazon `ca-central-1`) | The database and your profile photo | **Canada** |
| Netlify | The app's own files — HTML, images, code | Served worldwide; **contains no personal data** |
| Stripe | Payment records | Ireland and the United States |
| Our email provider | The single message sent to a parent about payment | Canada or the United States |

Choosing a Canadian database region was deliberate. Ontario school boards ask
where student data is stored, and for us the answer is Canada.

## 5. Who can see your information

- **You** — everything about yourself.
- **Your tutor**, if you are in their class — your name, your progress, your
  scores and your weak topics. Not your password, not your payment details.
- **An administrator at STEM Labs**, who can see accounts and subscription
  status in order to run the service and provide support.
- **Anyone you give a share link to.** See the next section — this one is
  worth reading properly.

Nobody else. We do not share information with schools, school boards, or any
other company, and we do not respond to informal requests for student
information. A court order or a legal obligation is the only exception, and we
will tell you if that ever happens unless we are forbidden to.

## 6. Share links, in plain terms

You can create a link that shows your report to a parent or guardian without
them needing an account. It is important that you understand exactly what that
means:

- The link contains a long random token. **Anyone who has the link can see
  that report**, whether you meant to give it to them or not.
- The report shows your **first name only**, your grades, your progress and
  your weak topics. It never shows your email address or your surname.
- You can see how many times it has been opened.
- **You can revoke it at any time**, from My Report. Revoking it stops the old
  link working immediately.

Treat a share link like a house key: give it to people you trust, and change
the lock if you lose track of it.

## 7. Students under 18

Astro Math Assist is built for students in Grades 9 to 12, most of whom are
between 14 and 18 years old.

- **If you are under 13**, a parent or guardian must set up and consent to your
  account. Please ask them to email us before signing up.
- **If you are 13 or older**, you can consent for yourself, but we strongly
  encourage you to tell a parent or guardian that you are using the app —
  particularly before you fill in the Astro+ form, which asks for their
  details.
- **A parent or guardian can ask us at any time** for a copy of their child's
  information, or for it to be deleted. We will verify who they are before
  acting.

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 who have signed
up without a parent. If we discover we have, we delete it.

## 8. How long we keep things

| What | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Your account and practice history | As long as your account exists |
| Practice history after you press "Reset my progress" | The old rows stay but are excluded from every score and report. Ask us if you want them removed outright |
| Enrolment forms with a parent's contact details | 12 months, then deleted |
| Payment records | 7 years, because Canadian tax law requires it |
| Rate-limit counters | 30 days |

**When you close your account, we delete your profile, your practice history,
your photo and your notes.** What survives is the payment record, and only
because we are legally required to keep it.

## 9. Your rights

Under Canada's *Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act*
(PIPEDA) you have the right to:

- **See** what we hold about you, and get a copy of it.
- **Correct** anything that is wrong.
- **Withdraw consent** and have your information deleted, subject to the legal
  retention above.
- **Complain**, to us first and then to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner
  of Canada at priv.gc.ca if we do not resolve it.

Email **stemlabs.ca@gmail.com** to exercise any of these. There is no charge,
and we will not make it difficult.

## 10. How your information is protected

Some specifics, rather than a general promise:

- Every connection uses HTTPS.
- The database enforces access rules **at the row level**, inside the database
  itself, not in the app. A student's account can only ever read that
  student's rows, even if the app has a bug.
- **Correct answers never leave the server.** Grading happens in the database.
  This exists to stop cheating, but it also means the answer key is not
  sitting in anyone's browser.
- Passwords are hashed by Supabase Auth. They are not recoverable, by us or by
  anyone else.
- Access to the administrator account is limited to the people who run STEM
  Labs.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise. If information
is ever exposed in a way that creates a real risk of significant harm, we will
tell the people affected and the Privacy Commissioner, as PIPEDA requires.

you in the app before the change takes effect. We will not quietly broaden
what we collect.
