1. What this is

Astro Math Assist gives Ontario high-school students multiple-choice practice across Grades 9 to 12, along with lessons, practice tests, and a report they can share with a parent or tutor.

It is a study aid. It is not a course, it is not accredited, it does not award credits, and using it is not a substitute for attending school or doing the work your teacher sets. Nothing in it is a prediction of what you will score on a real test.

2. Who may use it

  • You need an account, and you need to give a real email address.
  • If you are under 13, a parent or guardian has to set the account up and consent to it.
  • Give us accurate information. Telling us the wrong grade means you get the wrong questions, which only wastes your own time.
  • Your account is yours alone. Do not share your password or let someone else practise under your name — your report is meant to describe you, and it stops meaning anything if it describes two people.
