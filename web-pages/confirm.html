Weekly maths reports

A student has asked for you to receive a short weekly summary of their maths practice. Nothing is sent until you agree here.

What you would receive

  • How much they practised, and on how many days
  • Which topics they worked on and how those went
  • The specific mistakes they keep making, in plain words

One email a week, on Sunday evening, and only in weeks when they actually practised. They can also send you one themselves.

What you would not

No questions, no answers they gave, and no school marks. This is a practice app, not a test, and the report is meant to tell you what to help with rather than what they got wrong.

Every email has a one-click link to stop. You can also ask the student to remove you at any time, and they can see who is on their list.
